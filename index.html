Features

Everything you need to track your watchlist

Watchlist management

Organise titles into statuses — Watching, Completed, Plan to Watch, On Hold, or Dropped. Always know where you stand.

Episode-level progress

Track exactly which seasons and episodes you've seen for every TV show. Pick up right where you left off.

Ratings & reviews

Score any title on a numeric scale and write a short review. Build a personal record of everything you've watched.

Personalised recommendations

Set your favourite genres and languages. WatchLog suggests titles matched to your taste, not a generic trending list.

Watch history & stats

Timestamped viewing logs, progress percentages, and viewing statistics — see how much you've actually watched.

Search & discover

Instantly search millions of movies and shows from the TMDB catalogue and browse what's trending right now.
